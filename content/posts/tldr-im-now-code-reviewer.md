This is my own opinion. Thanks to Matt Pocock, I'm now a code reviewer.

Here's the thing. I never liked AI, but these days I use it with one condition. I refuse to let it work alone, and I have to be the Human in the Loop (HITL).

That condition didn't come out of nowhere because programming on my own is draining. I take the requirements, explain everything to people who don't write code, build what they ask for, then deal with the complaints. By the end of it my head hurts, but the user just sees the product shipped and nothing broke, and nobody counts the headaches or the sleepless nights.

Meanwhile, I keep seeing vibecoders on social media bragging about what their AI built, and I'm not impressed. I used to think I didn't need any of it, that I could handle everything myself, until I asked myself what all this was even for. Just a low paycheck and daily exhaustion. Somewhere along the way I gave my soul to harnesses, models, and agents.

So I started looking for the right AI provider, one that fit my tight budget but still gave decent results. Some people treat AI like an AFK machine, and my wallet would cry if I copied them, so instead of burning tokens like that, I stay in the loop. It takes longer, sure, but at least I know what code my agents are writing.

Then I found Matt Pocock's skills, and they were exactly what I needed. There's a grilling session that pushes back on my biased prompts and turns them into specs and tickets, and when the agent implements the code, it runs the tests too. After that, the reviewing is on me, and I keep cycling until every feature is done.

So that's where I'm at, trying to treat AI like a coworker and still figuring it out as I go.
